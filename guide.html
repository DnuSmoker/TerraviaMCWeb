Getting Started

Welcome to TerraviaMC! When you first join the server, you will spawn at our central hub. From here, you can venture out into the Earth map using wild teleports or run directly out of spawn.

Quick Tip: Use /map to open our live Dynmap web interface in your browser to find unsettled land before travelling!

Towns & Claims

Towns are protected regions where you and your friends can build safely without fear of griefing or chest theft.

  • /t new [Name] - Founds a new town at your current location (Requires Gold).
  • /t claim - Claims the 16x16 chunk you are currently standing in.
  • /t deposit [Amount] - Adds gold to your town bank to pay daily upkeep.

Nations & Diplomacy

Nations are alliances formed between multiple towns under a single leadership. Forming or joining a nation allows you to participate in global politics and large-scale warfare.

  • /n new [Name] - Creates a nation centered around your capital town.
  • /n add [Town] - Invites another town mayor to join your nation.
  • /n ally [Nation] - Forms a formal alliance with another nation.

Economy & Gold

Our server economy is backed by Gold Ingots. You can earn gold by mining, trading with other players, or selling goods through player-run shops inside towns.

Siege War

Wars are declared between nations on Discord prior to in-game conflict. Players place battle banners outside rival towns to initiate siege battles over territories.

Dynmap & Coordinates

Use our real-time dynamic web map to track town borders, nation territories, online players, and land terrain across the 1:500 Earth scale map.

Skills & Progression

Levelling up your mcMMO abilities grants passive buffs for mining, herbalism, woodcutting, archery, and swordsmanship as you play.

Support & Tickets

If you encounter bugs, need alt account registration, or suspect a rulebreaker, please open a support ticket on our official Discord server.
