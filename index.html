Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join TerraviaMC?

Launch Minecraft Java Edition (version 1.20+), navigate to Multiplayer, click Add Server, and enter TerraviaMC.org.

Is the server Bedrock compatible?

No. Bedrock players can't join as The server is only java compatible.

Are there Moderators?

Yes! Our skilled and talented moderators can help you if someone breaks the rules, someone claim-blocks you, and more!

How does the Earth Map work?

Our server features a custom 1:500 scale real-world Earth map where you can claim real geography to construct your town or nation.

Is TerraviaMC Pay-to-Win?

No. All gameplay progression and town land claims are made using the in-game currency (Gold). Which cannot be bought online—players have to mine, vote, or trade to earn gold.

Does TerraviaMC Have Ranks?

Yes! Players can purchase Ranks to add a special look to their Nametag or show a token of appreciation towards the TMC Team! (No special items are given, only cosmetics such as tags.)
